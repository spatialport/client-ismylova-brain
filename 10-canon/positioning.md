# Positioning

The only room in Miami combining wardrobe, wellness, culture and the table under one founder-hosted roof. Competitive map: concept boutiques (The Webster, Alchemist) do episodes not programmes; members clubs ($3–10k/yr) sell hospitality without wardrobe; Alo proves store-community at scale without intimacy; the maisons produce spectacle without relationship.
