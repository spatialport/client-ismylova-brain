# Company

IS MY LOVA is an independent luxury fashion boutique in Miami Beach. Two locations: 400 Alton Road TH-2A (two floors plus terrace) and a corner at The Setai (Museum Beach, steps from The Bass). Website: ismylova.com. Legal entity: tbd.

The house carries ~45 designer labels including Magda Butrym, Coperni, Self-Portrait, Nanushka, Alberta Ferretti, Moschino, Alex Perry, Aya Muse, D'Estrée, Acler, Goyard, Lapima, Zeynep Tosun, The Garment and SIMKHAI, plus an own label.
